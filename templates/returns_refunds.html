Our 15-Day Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase from MyStore. If you are not happy with your item, you may return it within 15 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Eligibility for Return

  • The item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition that you received it.
  • It must be in the original packaging with all tags still attached.
  • Certain items are exempt from returns (e.g., clearance items, intimates, and customized items).

How to Return an Item

  1. Go to your Dashboard and find your order under "Orders".
  2. Select "Return Item" next to the product you wish to return.
  3. Fill out the quick return form describing the issue (e.g., Wrong Size, Defective, Changed Mind).
  4. Print the prepaid shipping label sent to your email and attach it to the package.
  5. Drop off the package at any partnered logistics center or request a pickup.

Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected (typically within 48 hours of reaching our warehouse), we will send you an email to notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved, a credit will automatically be applied to your credit card or original method of payment within 3-7 business days.
