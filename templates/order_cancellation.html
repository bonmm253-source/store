How to Cancel Your Order

We process orders rapidly to ensure fast delivery. You can cancel your order directly from your dashboard only before it has been shipped.

Steps to Cancel

  1. Log into your account and navigate to My Dashboard.
  2. Go to the Orders section and find the order you want to cancel.
  3. If the status is still Pending or Processing, click the "Cancel Order" button next to it.
  4. Select a reason for cancellation and confirm.

What if my order is already shipped?

Once an order has been handed over to our delivery partners (Status: Shipped), it can no longer be cancelled through the dashboard.

However, you can simply refuse delivery when the courier contacts you, and the item will be returned to us automatically. If you prepaid via card, a refund will be processed upon the item's return.
